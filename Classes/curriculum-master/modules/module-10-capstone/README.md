# 🎓 Module 10 — Demo Day
> **Estimated Time:** Multi-session capstone | **AutoGrader:** Enabled

## 🎯 The Mission

Design, build, and present a complete IoT project that:
1. **Solves a real problem** in your home, school, or community
2. **Uses at least 2 sensors** (temperature, light, moisture, buttons, etc.)
3. **Has at least 1 actuator** (LED, servo, buzzer, relay, OLED display)
4. **Includes serial output** for data logging or debugging
5. **Is well-documented** with comments and a wiring diagram
6. **You can explain it** in a 3–5 minute presentation

## Suggested Capstone Projects
| Project | Sensors | Actuators |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| 🌱 Smart Greenhouse | Soil moisture, DHT22, LDR | Relay/pump, OLED, LEDs |
| 🔐 Room Security | PIR motion, reed switch | Buzzer, OLED |
| 🎵 Digital Instrument | Potentiometer, buttons | Buzzer, LEDs |
| 🌤️ Weather Alert | DHT22, BME280, LDR | OLED, LED indicators |
