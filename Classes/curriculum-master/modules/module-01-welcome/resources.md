## 📝 Cheat Sheets

- [Arduino Cheat Sheet](https://www.arduino.cc/en/uploads/Main/arduino_cheat_sheet.pdf) — One-page reference
- `setup()` — Runs once when the board powers on
- `loop()` — Runs over and over forever
- `Serial.begin(9600)` — Start serial communication
- `Serial.println("Hello")` — Send text to the Serial Monitor
